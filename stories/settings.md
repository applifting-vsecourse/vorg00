# Settings

As a user I want to update my profile and preferred mood so my posts identify me correctly.

- Settings is in the user menu and starts with the saved values.
- Username: 3–30 letters, digits, dots or underscores, unique ignoring case. Conflicts appear at the field without losing the draft.
- Display name is required (up to 100 characters); bio is optional (up to 160).
- Default mood is none, happy, sad, angry or silly. It preselects the new-post form.
- Saving confirms success and refreshes the header and existing posts. Discard restores saved values.
- All inputs have labels, keyboard navigation and inline errors; Enter submits and the first invalid field receives focus.

Out of scope: password, e-mail, avatar and account deletion.
