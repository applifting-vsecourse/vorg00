# Optional quack mood

As someone writing a quack, I want to optionally choose a mood so readers understand its tone.

## Acceptance criteria

- Choose one of Happy, Sad, Angry, or Silly; clicking the selected mood clears it.
- New and existing posts without a mood keep their usual appearance.
- A saved mood appears with an icon and name and survives reloading.
- Successful posting resets the form; failed posting keeps the draft.
- Mood choices work with a keyboard and indicate their selected state.

Out of scope: editing moods, custom moods, and reactions.
